# Launcher artwork sources

All four assets were drawn anew on 2026-09-30 using OpenAI's built-in `image_gen.imagegen` image generation tool. The built-in tool does not expose its underlying model identifier; no CLI/API model was selected. They are original artwork inspired by the original Tyrian and Tyrian 2000 box covers and their respective logos. Those references were inspected only for style, composition, palette, lettering and mood; they were not passed as image inputs, traced, pasted, or included in the repository. No publisher names, ratings, taglines, trademark marks or watermarks are reproduced.

The generated panels were resampled to 960×1080 RGB PNG. The generated title images retain genuine alpha transparency and were fitted to 800×320 RGBA PNG. Local sizing and PNG encoding use macOS Core Graphics/ImageIO. PNG IDAT data is recompressed losslessly with zlib level 9. Background RGB values discard only their least significant bit (at most 1/255 per channel) to keep the embedded files below 1.5 MB; the PNGs remain 8-bit RGB and the titles retain their generated alpha. The art is licensed with the repository.
